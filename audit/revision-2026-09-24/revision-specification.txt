FINAL OBJECTIVE
======================================================================

The revised manuscript should leave a specialist reader with the following understanding:

The paper is not proposing a new LDPC decoder and is not claiming that multiple
subexponentials are necessary.

Rather, it develops a precise certification architecture in which asynchronous
resource-sensitive rewriting can be related exactly to operational peeling despite
delayed propagation. A normalization theorem recovers the fully updated operational
state, an event-labelled adequacy theorem relates executions and traces, and residual
linear algebra supplies independently checkable success or infeasibility certificates.

SELL provides a disciplined specification vocabulary for this architecture, while the
paper explicitly determines the limits of what that SELL structure contributes.

The mathematical centerpiece is the exact trace-adequacy result, not the mere use
of SELL.
